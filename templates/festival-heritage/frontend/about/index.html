About Diyandi

A festival made from many stories.

Diyandi is Iligan's living expression of faith, heritage, and shared celebration.

  • Rooted in Higaonon language
  • Celebrated across Iligan
  • Centered on community
DIYANDI

The meaning

A deeper name for a shared celebration.

DiyandiCelebration · Gathering · Thanksgiving

Diyandi is a Higaonon word associated with celebration and thanksgiving. In Iligan, that meaning becomes a contemporary city gathering where devotion, culture, enterprise, and hospitality meet in public life.

ROOTS

Shared heritage

Three traditions.
One civic story.

01

Higaonon
