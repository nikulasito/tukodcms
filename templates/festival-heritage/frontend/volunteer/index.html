Bring your energy to Diyandi.

  • Open to ages 18+

DIYANDI

Why volunteer

Small roles create a better festival.

Volunteers are often the first people visitors meet. Your time can make directions clearer, event spaces calmer, cultural information more useful, and the entire celebration more welcoming.
