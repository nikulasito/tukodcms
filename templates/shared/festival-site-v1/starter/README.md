# Generic Festival Starter

This starter is the generic, provisionable frontend scaffold for TukodPH festival websites.

- No festival-specific names, branding, dates, places, or imagery are included here.
- Tenant-specific branding and content should come from the CMS after provisioning.
- Design templates may layer their own theme tokens and components on top of this shared structure.
- Templates may also provide their own page routes, section anchors, and layout-aware CMS renderers when the shared scaffold is not enough.

## Included routes

- Shared baseline routes:
- `/`
- `/about/`
- `/events/`
- `/contact/`
- `/sponsor/`

Template-specific routes may extend or replace the baseline set. Examples in this repo include `/volunteer/`, `/plan-your-festival-days/`, and `/plan-your-visit/`.
