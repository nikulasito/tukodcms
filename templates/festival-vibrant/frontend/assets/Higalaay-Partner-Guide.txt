Higalaay Festival Partner Guide

Thank you for your interest in partnering with Higalaay Festival.

Available Recognition Tiers
- Platinum Partner
- Diamond Partner
- Gold Partner
- Silver Partner
- Bronze Partner
- Donor

Typical Partner Visibility
- Logo placement on festival partner pages and presentation materials
- Brand recognition in selected festival communications
- Possible on-ground visibility depending on package, venue, and city guidelines
- Coordination with the festival team for activations, booths, or community support

Helpful Details to Prepare
- Company or organization name
- Contact person and role
- Preferred partnership tier
- Partnership goals
- Proposed activation or support
- Logo files and brand usage guidelines
